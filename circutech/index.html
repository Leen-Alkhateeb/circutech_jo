Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Learn more about secure device trade-ins, resale, and certified recycling.

How can I prepare my device before recycling?

Back up your personal files, sign out of all cloud accounts, and perform a factory reset. Always remove SIM and memory cards before handing over your device.

Which electronic devices can I submit?

Our platform handles smartphones, laptops, tablets, smart TVs, networking routers, and household appliances. Acceptance depends on condition.

How is the estimated device value calculated?

Estimates factor in the device category, release year, functional condition, and market resale demand.

Can corporate entities request ESG sustainability reports?

Yes! Businesses can partner with our team to obtain verified carbon offset reports and ITAD destruction certificates.
